# Project Brief

## Company

TrackFlow is a B2B logistics company founded in 2009 with operations in Monterrey, Mexico and Zaragoza, Spain. It serves mid-sized e-commerce brands in fashion, electronics, and cosmetics through warehouse management, last-mile delivery, and reverse logistics.

## Business Problem

The current corporate website is outdated and does not explain TrackFlow's binational footprint or the value of its three logistics services. Sales is receiving unqualified inbound requests by email and needs a structured intake flow to capture better lead data from companies evaluating logistics outsourcing.

## Milestone 4 Objective

Turn the monorepo into a stable delivery base for future AI-driven engineering work by:

- establishing persistent agent context through a memory bank,
- defining repo-level agent workflow and protected areas,
- documenting at least one reusable agent skill,
- migrating the corporate website into a reusable Next.js application,
- preparing an `/internal-app` area that already surfaces business logic from Milestone 2 without copying that code.

## Product Outcomes

- Public website at `/` presenting TrackFlow's services, coverage, differentiation, and sales contact points.
- Lead capture experience at `/contacto` with the validation rules defined in `CONTEXT.md`.
- Internal workspace at `/internal-app` ready for future milestones and already connected to TrackFlow's TypeScript utilities.

## Non-Negotiable Constraints

- All content and workflows must stay aligned with TrackFlow's Mexico/Spain logistics scenario.
- No duplication of the Milestone 2 business logic module.
- The implementation must be easy to run in GitHub Codespaces.
