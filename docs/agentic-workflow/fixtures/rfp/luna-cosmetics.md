# Request for Proposal — Luna Cosmetics

**Client / Brand:** Luna Cosmetics  
**Company type:** DTC cosmetics brand  
**Origin / Country:** Los Angeles, United States (US)  
**Document type:** Request for Proposal (RFP)

## Summary

Luna Cosmetics is requesting a commercial logistics proposal from TrackFlow to outsource **warehousing** and **last-mile delivery** for the United States market. Reverse logistics is out of scope for this RFP.

## Services requested

- Warehousing / storage, picking and packing
- Last-mile delivery across the US
- Real-time inventory visibility preferred

## Volume and timeline

- Estimated volume: ~5,000 orders/month
- Deadline for proposal: 20 days from receipt
- Budget range: to be discussed after operational fit

## Requirements notes

We need TrackFlow to confirm storage capacity for cosmetics SKUs, onboarding lead time, per-shipment pricing, available carriers by destination, and an on-time delivery SLA commitment.
